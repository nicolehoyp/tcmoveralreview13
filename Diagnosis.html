01診斷導論Introduction to TCM Diagnosis

學習目標 Learning Outcomes

  • Analyze chief complaints to initiate the relevant assessment for a TCM health intake.
  • Compare the concepts of disease, symptom, and syndrome according to TCM methodology.
  • Determine the most relevant TCM diagnostic framework to diagnose a patient.
  • Determine syndromes to make a TCM diagnosis for a clinical case scenario.
  • 分析主訴，以啟動中醫健康問診中相應的評估。
  • 依中醫方法學，比較「疾病（病）」、「症狀」與「證候（證）」三個概念。
  • 判斷最適合該病人的中醫辨證框架。
  • 針對臨床案例辨別證候，做出中醫診斷。

診斷三大原則 Principles in Diagnosis

必記1. Holism 整體觀

The human body is an organic whole. All tissues and organs are related (support, nourishment and function). In diagnosis, the practitioner must understand how a symptom (the branch/標) in one location may be caused by an issue somewhere else (the root/本).

The body is in relationship with Nature. Health changes from season to season, in damp or hot weather, day and night, and with diet and exercise. We must understand the changing nature of symptoms.

必記1. 整體觀念

人體是一個有機整體。所有組織與臟腑彼此關聯（支持、濡養、功能相依）。診斷時，醫者必須理解某一部位的症狀（標）可能是他處問題（本）所致。

人與自然相應（天人相應）。健康隨季節、濕熱氣候、晝夜、飲食與運動而變化；須掌握症狀隨之變動的規律。

必記2. Four Diagnostic Methods 四診

Practitioners use four methods to gather evidence: Looking (望), Listening & Smelling (聞), Asking (問), and Touching (切).

The evidence gathered can be generalised into Symptoms and Signs, and then interpreted to become a Syndrome / Pattern (證).

必記2. 四診

醫者運用四種方法收集資料：望、聞、問、切。

所收集的資料可歸納為症狀與體徵，再加以解讀而形成證候（證）。

必記3. Disease (病) + Syndrome (證)

A TCM diagnosis must consist of two parts = Disease + Syndrome.

Disease (Bìng 病): a category of illness with a general presentation that does not vary between patients — usually named after the most troublesome symptom/sign. E.g. Diarrhea.

Syndrome (Zhèng 證): the varying signs and symptoms that differ between patients help define the syndrome. E.g. acute diarrhea with fever, or chronic loose stool after certain foods.

必記3. 病 + 證

中醫診斷必須包含兩部分＝病＋證。

疾病（病 Bìng）：一類具有共同表現、且在不同病人間大致相同的病症，通常以病人最困擾的症狀/體徵命名。例：泄瀉。

證候（證 Zhèng）：同一疾病在不同病人身上會出現不同的症徵，這些差異界定了「證」。例：急性泄瀉兼發熱，或食用特定食物後的慢性便溏。

病＋證＝診斷（範例）：泄瀉＝大腸濕熱｜肩痛＝經絡氣血阻滯｜不孕＝腎陰陽兩虛｜哮喘＝腎肺氣虛兼痰阻。 Diarrhea = Damp-Heat in the LI · Shoulder Pain = Channel obstruction of Qi & Blood · Infertility = KI Yin & Yang Def · Asthma = KI & LU Qi Def with Phlegm.

症狀 vs 體徵 Symptoms vs Signs

Symptoms — subjective, therefore described by the patient. E.g. headache, pain, thirst.

Signs — objective, therefore observed by the practitioner. E.g. pale complexion, red tongue, wiry pulse.

症狀 Symptoms——主觀，由病人自述。例：頭痛、疼痛、口渴。

體徵 Signs——客觀，由醫者觀察。例：面色蒼白、舌紅、脈弦。

「證」的組成 Defining a Syndrome

A syndrome is a summary of a disease at a certain stage, determined by: Cause, Location, Nature, Pathogenesis, and relevant s/sx.

Example — Damp-Heat in the Liver & Gallbladder:
Location: Liver & Gallbladder · Nature: Excess & Hot · Pathogenesis: Damp-Heat accumulation.

證是疾病在某一階段的概括，由以下要素界定：病因、病位、病性、病機、相關症徵。

範例——肝膽濕熱：
病位：肝、膽 · 病性：實、熱 · 病機：濕熱蘊結。

範例病案 Case Study — 貫穿全模組

Case · 女性 36 歲（endomorph 內胚型／偏胖體質）

Chief Complaint: High-pitched ringing in the ears, following an episode of temporary hearing loss.

History: Argument with her daughter 2 days ago → temporary hearing loss + severe headache; since yesterday, hearing loss changed to high-pitch tinnitus. 3-yr history of high blood pressure. Family: mother diabetic, father hypertensive.

Symptoms: tinnitus worse with unhappy/stressful thoughts; headache; irritability; difficulty falling asleep, active mind, frequent waking; bitter taste; thirst with desire for cold drinks; dark yellow urine; constipation.

Signs: red complexion; talkative & loud; difficulty focusing; endomorphic build; Tongue red body, yellow coating; Pulse wiry (xian) & rapid (shuo).

主訴：耳中高頻鳴響，發作前曾一過性聽力喪失。

病史：兩天前與女兒爭吵 → 短暫聽力喪失＋劇烈頭痛；昨起轉為高頻耳鳴。高血壓病史 3 年。家族史：母糖尿病、父高血壓。

症狀：耳鳴於情緒不悅／壓力時加重；頭痛；煩躁易怒；入睡困難、睡中思緒活躍、頻醒；口苦；口渴喜冷飲；小便深黃；便秘。

體徵：面紅；多言聲高；注意力難集中；體型偏胖；舌紅、苔黃；脈弦而數。

臨床提示：此案為典型肝火（肝陽/肝膽火）上擾之象——弦數脈、面紅、口苦、便秘尿黃、情志觸發，貫穿本模組供辨病＋辨證練習。

回家作業：建立病案 Homework — Case Record

Interview a family member or friend and create a case record using these headings:

  • General info (age, sex, body habitus)
  • Chief Complaint(s) — what & how long?
  • Symptoms · Signs
  • Personal medical history & medication (incl. supplements)
  • Family medical history
  • Other relevant info (lifestyle, diet, exercise, environment, occupation)

Then create a TCM Disease (from Appendix L) → choose a differentiation method → identify a syndrome → determine treatment principles → decide on herbs/acupuncture/other.

訪談一位家人或朋友，依下列標題建立病案：

  • 一般資料（年齡、性別、體型）
  • 主訴——是什麼、多久了？
  • 症狀 · 體徵
  • 個人病史與用藥（含保健品）
  • 家族病史
  • 其他相關資訊（生活型態、飲食、運動、環境、職業）

接著訂出中醫病名（依附錄 L）→ 選擇辨證方法 → 確立證候 → 制訂治則 → 決定用藥／針灸／其他治療。

02四診Four Diagnostic Methods — 望・聞・問・切
2·1望診Looking / Inspection

望診概說 General — What we "look" at

Looking encompasses observations made with our eyes. The moment the patient walks in, the diagnostic process has begun — some observations are obvious, others subtle.

We look at: General appearance · Movement · Shen (神) · Complexion · Excretions · Tongue.

望診即以目觀察。病人一踏進診間，診斷即已開始——有些徵象明顯，有些細微。

觀察項目：整體外觀 · 動態 · 神 · 面色 · 排出物 · 舌象。

1. 整體外觀 General Appearance

As the patient walks in, note: (1) body shape & size; (2) movement as they walk toward you; (3) handshake, eye contact, ease of interaction.

  • Strong & robust = strong Qi, good Jing → if ill, recovers quickly.
  • Weak & frail = Deficient → heals more slowly.
  • Overweight = Excess Dampness or Qi Def → check SP (also LU/KI for water metabolism).
  • Thin or sallow = Yin or Blood Def.
  • Outgoing, talkative, aggressive, irritable = Yang-like pattern.
  • Passive, quiet, easily affected = Yin-like pattern.

病人進門時留意：(1) 體型大小；(2) 走向你時的動態；(3) 握手、眼神接觸、互動是否自在。

  • 強壯結實＝氣盛、精足 → 若病，痊癒快。
  • 虛弱羸瘦＝虛證 → 病癒較慢。
  • 體胖＝濕盛或氣虛 → 查脾（水液代謝亦查肺、腎）。
  • 瘦弱或萎黃＝陰虛或血虛。
  • 外向、多言、好強、易怒＝陽性（陽型）證。
  • 被動、寡言、易受影響＝陰性（陰型）證。

2. 動態 Movement

  • Heavy, deliberate, forceful = Excess
  • Frail, weak, cautious = Deficiency
  • Quick = Heat
  • Slow = Cold
  • 沉重、刻意、有力＝實證
  • 虛弱、無力、謹慎＝虛證
  • 動作快＝熱證
  • 動作慢＝寒證

3. 神 Shen (Spirit)

必記Shen is the general manifestation of life (vitality), mental states and activities. It is seen through facial expression, complexion, and vitality in the eyes. Its material base is Essence/Blood and it is associated with the Heart.

必記神是生命力（生機）、精神狀態與活動的整體表現，透過面部表情、面色與眼神的活力顯現。其物質基礎為精、血，並與心相關。

  • Good Shen 得神：mental acuity, presence, healthy vitality.
  • Lack of Shen 少神：dispirited, pale complexion.
  • Loss of Shen 失神：dull eyes, absence of presence, weak breathing / dyspnoea.
  • False Shen 假神：a sudden spurt of vitality before death — "the last flicker of a candle." KI energy about to separate; Yang floats to the surface. Death occurs with separation of Yin & Yang.
  • Shen derangement 神亂：unstable mental function — manic episodes, hallucinations; whites visible above the iris.
  • 得神：思維敏銳、有存在感、生機健旺。
  • 少神：精神不振、面色蒼白。
  • 失神：目光呆滯、缺乏存在感、呼吸微弱或氣促。
  • 假神：臨終前突現生機——「燈燭將熄前的迴光返照」。腎氣將絕，陽浮於外；陰陽離決則死。
  • 神亂：精神功能不穩——狂躁、幻覺；可見「白多黑少」露白眼。
如何察神：眼（無神、呆滯）、腦（欠清明）、面（表情與情緒不符）、語（語無倫次、失神、失去思路）、態（笨拙、姿態怪異）、行（反應不當，如喪禮發笑；過度或不合宜地笑）。健康之笑應由胸而發、深沉「爽朗」；若為喉間乾笑、未連於真心，則為神之病。 Assess Shen via Eyes · Brain · Face · Speech · Posture · Behaviour. A healthy laugh comes from the chest; a throaty laugh disconnected from the Heart signals a Shen problem.

4. 面色 Complexion

Assess colour and lustre (ask patients to come without make-up/creams). Qi & Blood of the channels pour upward into the face: a fine complexion = healthy Yang Qi (heals easily); a lustrous skin = healthy Yin/Blood.

望面色須看顏色與光澤（囑病人勿化妝、勿塗乳霜）。經脈氣血上榮於面：面色佳＝陽氣健旺（易癒）；膚有光澤＝陰血充足。

5. 排出物 Excretions

Often relies on the patient's report (sometimes directly observed). Know what "normal" is, then assess by quantity, consistency, colour (and sometimes temperature). Consider how each changes with Heat, Cold, Dampness, and Qi/Blood/Yin/Yang disruptions.

Discuss: Phlegm · Vomit · Sweat · Urine · Stool.

多依病人陳述（有時可直接觀察）。須先知「常」，再依量、質、色（有時含溫度）評估。思考各排出物在寒、熱、濕及氣血陰陽失調下如何變化。

討論項目：痰 · 嘔吐物 · 汗 · 小便 · 大便。

6. 舌診 Tongue Diagnosis

必記Tongue diagnosis is unique and important, and relatively easy to learn given its objective nature. It reveals:

  • State of the internal organs — location of disease
  • State of Qi & Blood — Deficiency or stagnation
  • Presence of pathogenic factors — Heat, Cold, Damp, Phlegm, Wind
  • The 8 Principles (Heat/Cold, Excess/Def, Yin/Yang)

必記舌診獨特而重要，因其客觀，相對易學。可反映：

  • 臟腑狀態——病位所在
  • 氣血狀態——虛或滯
  • 病邪之有無——熱、寒、濕、痰、風
  • 八綱（寒熱、虛實、陰陽）

Technique & Lighting 手法與光線

Best observed in sunlight (indoors: halogen). After ~15 s the tongue blood stagnates and turns purplish, so the patient should not protrude the tongue > 10–15 s at a time.

Follow a system (order by clinical importance, per Maciocia 2019): 1) body colour → 2) body shape → 3) coating → 4) spirit.

External factors: coloured food/drink/candy, spicy food, smoking, medications.

手法與光線

宜於日光下觀察（室內用鹵素燈）。伸舌約 15 秒後舌血瘀滯轉紫，故每次伸舌不超過 10–15 秒。

按系統觀察（Maciocia 2019 依臨床重要性排序）：1) 舌質色 → 2) 舌形 → 3) 舌苔 → 4) 舌神。

外在干擾：有色食物/飲料/糖果、辛辣食物、吸菸、藥物（染苔）。

1) Body Colour 舌質色

Reflects Yin organs & Blood, Heat/Cold, Yin/Yang Def. Include papillae and sublingual veins. Normal = pale-red.

2) Body Shape 舌形

Reflects Excess/Deficiency. Assess whole shape, thickness, suppleness, surface, movement. Normal = supple, not swollen/thin, no cracks.

3) Coating 舌苔

Reflects Yang organs (esp. Stomach), Excess/Def, Hot/Cold, pathogens. Formed by ST during digestion — patchy/"rootless" coat = weak ST Qi. Normal = thin & white.

4) Spirit 舌神

Brightness, sheen, vitality → reflects KI Essence & ability to recover. Spirited tongue = "fresh meat on a butcher's block"; lacking spirit = "old, dark, greyish, lifeless meat" (Maciocia 2019, p.222).

1) 舌質色

反映陰臟與血、寒熱、陰陽虛。須含舌乳頭與舌下絡脈。常色＝淡紅。

2) 舌形

反映虛實。察整體形狀、厚薄、柔軟度、表面、動態。正常＝柔軟、不胖不瘦、無裂紋。

3) 舌苔

反映陽腑（尤其胃）、虛實、寒熱、病邪。苔由胃於消化時所生——花剝或「無根苔」＝胃氣弱。正常＝薄白。

4) 舌神

明亮、光澤與生機 → 反映腎精及康復力。有神之舌如「砧板上新鮮之肉」；無神則如「陳舊、晦暗、灰白、無生氣之肉」。

舌面分區：舌尖＝心肺（上焦）；舌中＝脾胃（中焦）；舌根＝腎（下焦）；舌邊＝肝膽。特定分區之色變或形態變化，可對應相關臟腑之病變。 Tongue areas correspond to organs: tip = HT/LU, centre = SP/ST, root = KI, sides = LR/GB (Maciocia 2019, p.219).

  • Red 紅 with coating = Excess Heat; red without coating = Yin Def with Empty Heat.
  • Crimson 絳 with coating = Heat in Ying/Blood level (Excess); crimson without coating = Yin Def with Empty Fire (Def).
  • Purple 紫 = stagnation of Qi & Blood: purple+pale+moist = stasis d/t Yang Def; purple+red+dry = Excess Internal Heat; purple with dark macules = Blood stasis.

  • 淡白而瘦＝氣血兩虛；淡白而胖＝陽虛。
  • 紅而有苔＝實熱；紅而無苔＝陰虛內熱（虛熱）。
  • 絳（深紅）有苔＝熱入營血（實）；絳而無苔＝陰虛火旺（虛火）。
  • 紫＝氣血瘀滯：紫淡而潤＝陽虛致瘀；紫紅而乾＝內熱熾盛（多因飲食情志）；紫而見瘀斑＝血瘀。

Body Shape 舌形

  • Thin 瘦 & pale = Blood Def; thin & red (no coat) = Yin Def.
  • Swollen 胖大 = Damp/Phlegm; swollen+red = Damp-Heat/Fire; swollen+pale = Damp/Phlegm over a Deficiency.

  • 強硬＝內風或血瘀（紅＝熱；淡＝血虛生風或陽虛寒凝致瘀）。

2·3問診Asking

問診總論 "What's your story?"

Likely the most important diagnostic method — and the most difficult. Initial phase: make the patient comfortable, don't rush, follow the chief complaint, let them speak freely, listen to what most bothers them — this focuses treatment and usually determines the Disease.

1. 疼痛 Pain

"If the channels are free there is no pain; if the channels are obstructed there is pain."
「通則不痛，痛則不通。」
— 黃帝內經 Huang Di Nei Jing

必記Nature — Excess or Deficiency?

Excess 實：Excess factors block Qi & Blood in the channels. Usually sudden onset; "intense", "sharp", "distending". Causes: Qi/Blood stagnation, Interior Cold or Heat, food retention, Phlegm obstruction, invasion of external pathogens.

Deficiency 虛：Deficient substances → malnourished channels. Slower onset, lingering; dull or less intense. Causes: Qi/Blood Def, Fluid/Yin Def.

必記辨性——實或虛？

實：實邪阻滯經中氣血。多驟發；「劇烈」「銳利」「脹」。病因：氣血瘀滯、內寒或內熱、食積、痰阻、外邪侵襲。

虛：物質不足 → 經脈失養。多緩發、綿延；隱痛或較輕。病因：氣血虛、津液或陰虛。

Relieving / Aggravating Factors 緩解與加重

Relieved by: heat = Cold · cold = Heat · pressure = Def · eating = Def · bowel movement = Excess · vomiting = Excess · rest = Def · movement = Excess · lying down = Def · sitting up = Excess.

Aggravated by: pressure = Excess · eating = Excess · humidity = Damp · bowel movement = Def · vomiting = Def · rest = Excess · movement = Def.

緩解與加重因素

得…則緩：得熱＝寒 · 得涼＝熱 · 得按＝虛 · 得食＝虛 · 便後＝實 · 嘔後＝實 · 休息＝虛 · 活動＝實 · 平臥＝虛 · 坐起＝實。

遇…則甚：按壓＝實 · 進食＝實 · 潮濕＝濕 · 排便＝虛 · 嘔吐＝虛 · 休息＝實 · 活動＝虛。

By Cause 依病因

Qi Stagnation: bloating, fullness, distension, no fixed location; stress-related, comes & goes; common in abdomen/epigastrium.

Liver Yang Rising: Qi rebellion (severe stagnation); distending, throbbing headaches.

Blood Stasis: sharp, stabbing, severe, fixed location; head, chest, epigastrium, abdomen, Uterus.

Cold: cramping, colicky; aggravated by cold weather/food, relieved by heat; digestive tract, Uterus, joints.

Heat: burning; aggravated by heat, relieved by cold; often + Damp → heaviness.

EPFs: all 6 can cause pain — Wind (occipital HA, stiffness), Damp (heavy head/joints/abdomen). Food Retention: stomach/abdomen, full & intense, common in children.

3. 二便 Stool & Urine

Bowel function reflects SP & LI; movement of food is influenced by smooth LR Qi. Excess feels better after a BM; Deficiency feels worse.

Constipation 便秘

Relies on smooth LR Qi; LI extracts water as content moves. Qi stagnation → bowel stops, too much water extracted. Heat → dry, hard stool. Yin/Fluid Def → dry stool. Less commonly Qi/Yang Def → slow bowels, weak to push (stool not dry/hard).

大便反映脾與大腸；食物運行受肝氣疏泄影響。實證便後舒；虛證便後更差。

便秘

賴肝氣條達；大腸於傳導時吸收水分。氣滯 → 腸不動、水分吸收過多。熱 → 便乾硬。陰/津虛 → 便乾。較少見者為氣/陽虛 → 腸蠕動慢、無力推送（便不乾硬）。

Flatulence = LR Qi stagnation (± SP Qi). Foul = Damp-Heat in SP/ST; no smell = Internal Cold or SP Yang Def.

12. 寒、熱、發熱 Cold, Heat, Fever

  • Intolerance to Cold (畏寒 Wei Han) = Yang Deficiency, relieved by warmth.

  • 惡寒（Wu Han）＝寒邪為患，得溫不減。
  • 畏寒（Wei Han）＝陽虛，得溫則減。

「發熱／熱」可指體溫升高，亦可指主觀熱感。

Aversion to Cold WITH Fever (simultaneously) 惡寒發熱並見

Wind + pathogen enters the outer body, inhibits Wei Qi (Yang) → chills/aversion to Cold (stronger pathogen → more aversion). Zheng Qi mobilises to the surface → heat sensation (stronger fever → more anti-pathogenic Qi).

  • A) Severe aversion to Cold, slight fever + headache, body pain, NO sweat = External Cold (Excess) — Tai Yang Cold (Shang Han Lun).
  • B) High fever, slight aversion to Cold + sore throat, red tongue, rapid pulse, sweating = External Heat.
  • C) Aversion to Wind, slight fever + sweating = External Wind — Tai Yang Wind (Shang Han Lun).

惡寒發熱並見

風邪挾他邪入於淺表、遏抑衛陽 → 寒戰惡寒（邪盛則惡寒甚）。正氣趨表抗邪 → 熱感（熱甚則抗邪之氣足）。

  • A) 惡寒重、微發熱＋頭痛、身痛、無汗＝外感實寒——太陽傷寒（表實）。
  • B) 高熱、微惡寒＋咽痛、舌紅、脈數、有汗＝外感熱證。
  • C) 惡風、微熱＋有汗＝外感風證——太陽中風（表虛）。

Cold with NO Fever 但寒不熱

Excess Cold 實寒 (Cold pathogen enters Interior): shivering, pain (Cold constricts/blocks channels), objectively cold, not warmed by clothing. Pulse Strong/Full/Tight, usually Slow. Palpate abdomen: upper = ST, lower = LI/Uterus.

Empty Cold 虛寒 (Yang Def): chronic, feels cold often, may be cold to touch. Cold limbs = LU/HT Yang Def; cold abdomen = SP Yang Def; cold flanks/low back/knees/feet = KI Yang Def. Pulse weak, deep, slow. Better with extra clothing / socks to bed.

Qi Stagnation: Qi can't reach limbs → cold hands/feet (or just fingers/toes). Treat with Qi regulators, not warming/scattering herbs. Blood Def: HT Blood Def → cold hands/chest; LR Blood Def → cold feet.

但寒不熱

實寒（寒邪入裏）：寒戰、疼痛（寒性收引閉阻經絡）、觸之客觀發涼、加衣不暖。脈強/實/緊，多遲。按腹定位：上＝胃，下＝大腸/子宮。

虛寒（陽虛）：慢性、常畏寒、觸之亦涼。四肢冷＝肺/心陽虛；腹冷＝脾陽虛；脅、腰、膝、足冷＝腎陽虛。脈弱、沉、遲。加衣、穿襪入睡則舒。

氣滯：氣不達四末 → 手足（或僅指趾）冷。宜用理氣藥，非溫散寒之品。血虛：心血虛→手與胸冷；肝血虛→足冷。

Heat (Fever) 發熱

Heat = Excess/Deficient, External/Internal.

Interior Heat: may penetrate from outside or arise internally (diet/lifestyle/emotions). Hyper-functioning organ + heat sensation, irritability, restlessness, wants less clothing, cold water. Heat rises → chest/head/Heart; + Damp → settles lower.

Excess Internal Heat example — "4 Bigs" (四大症): fever >39°C, red face, thirst, profuse sweat, big pulse = Yang Ming Syndrome (Shang Han Lun) → White Tiger Decoction (白虎湯).

發熱

熱有虛實、內外之別。

裏熱：可由外入或內生（飲食/生活/情志）。臟腑功能亢進＋熱感、煩躁、不安、欲減衣、喜冷飲。熱性上炎→胸/頭/心；兼濕→下注。

裏實熱範例——四大症：發熱＞39°C、面赤、口渴、大汗、脈洪大＝陽明證（傷寒論）→ 白虎湯。

Middle Jiao		濕熱犯中焦，脾易為濕困、運化受損；因脾胃相關，胃受納亦礙腹部不適：脹、氣、消化不良、納呆；噁心嘔吐；腹瀉（或夾白黏液）；黃疸（膚目，濕熱盛時面呈晦濁暗黃）；發熱（微熱/午後熱/汗後減又升）；肢體沉重；口乾不欲飲；神識減退、乏力；尿頻灼灰、白和/或黃苔｜脈緩，或滑
下焦

Case 1 · 男 60 歲（高血壓）

Headache, dizziness, red eyes; headache at vertex; vertigo, bitter taste, yellow urine; tongue red w/ yellow coat; wiry & fast pulse. → Dx?

頭痛、眩暈、目赤；頭痛在巔頂；眩暈、口苦、尿黃；舌紅黃苔；脈弦數。→ 診斷？

Case 2 · 經絡選擇題

The kidney meridian connects to all these organs on its way up EXCEPT: Bladder / Heart / Spleen / Lung.  ｜  Which meridian does NOT reach the eye: GB / ST / LI / TW?

腎經上行「不」聯屬下列何臟腑：膀胱／心／脾／肺？ ｜ 下列何經「不」到眼：膽／胃／大腸／三焦？

答：腎經上行不聯屬脾（連膀胱、肝、肺、心）；大腸經不至眼（膽、胃、三焦皆抵目周）。

Alternating cold & fever ×1 month, bitter taste, dry throat, vertigo, chest & hypochondriac fullness, restlessness, vomiting, poor appetite; wiry pulse. → Dx?

Caught a cold 1 month ago; still mild fever, aversion to wind, neck rigidity & pain, spontaneous sweating, tiredness; floating & moderate pulse. → Dx?
