Introduction — A Brief History & the Practice Model of TCM

1.1中醫與針灸簡史A Brief History of TCM & Acupuncture

起源與早期工具Origins & earliest tools
English

TCM is thought to have originated in remote antiquity (the primitive era) in China. The Bian Shi (stone needle) was discovered in the New Stone Age (~7000–4000 B.C.) and is the earliest acupuncture tool unearthed — likely used for Gua Sha. Early bone needles also survive.

中文

中醫據信起源於中國遠古(原始時代)。砭石(石針)出土於新石器時代(約公元前 7000~4000 年),為現存最早的針灸工具,可能用於刮痧。另有早期骨針傳世。

春秋戰國(770–221 B.C.):《黃帝內經》Huang Di Nei Jing
English

The Huang Di Nei Jing (Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic) is considered the most important book in TCM history and lays out much of the theoretical system. This ~2000-year-old text introduces 160 points and 13 herbal formulas, written as a Q&A dialogue between the mythical Yellow Emperor and his ministers (notably Qi Bo). Two parts:

  • Suwen (Plain Questions) — how to live in harmony with the world/heavens to prevent disease and live long; lays the framework TCM still uses.
  • Lingshu (Spiritual Pivot) — the use of acupuncture to correct disharmony in the body.
中文

《黃帝內經》被視為中醫史上最重要的典籍,奠定大部分理論體系。此約兩千年前之書提出 160 個穴位與 13 首方劑,以黃帝與大臣(尤其岐伯)問答形式寫成,分兩部:

  • 素問——講述如何順應天地以預防疾病、延年益壽,建立中醫沿用至今的框架。
  • 靈樞——詳述以針灸矯正身體失調。
秦漢(221 B.C.–A.D. 220)Qin & Han
English

Nan Jing (Canon of Difficulties): developed and enriched the Nei Jing; most notably shifted TCM to taking the pulse only at the Cun Kou / wrist (radial) position.

中文

《難經》:發展並豐富《內經》;最重要的是使中醫脈診轉為僅取寸口/腕部(橈動脈)之獨取寸口法。

English

Shang Han Za Bing Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage), by Zhang Zhongjing, the "Sage of Medicine" — the first monograph on clinical medicine. It outlines the etiology, pathogenesis and treatment of infectious illnesses at each stage, using a syndrome differentiation called Liu Jing / Six Stages to track disease progression from onset to death. Contains 112 "classic formulas", still effective and widely used.

中文

《傷寒雜病論》,由「醫聖」張仲景所著——第一部臨床醫學專著。闡述外感熱病各階段之病因、病機與治療,以六經辨證追蹤疾病由發病至死亡的演變。收 112 首「經方」,至今仍有效且廣泛使用。

English

Jingui Yaolue (Essential Prescriptions from the Golden Cabinet): another seminal text by Zhang Zhongjing, outlining patterns and treatment of non-infectious illnesses.

中文

《金匱要略》:張仲景另一鉅著,論述雜病(非外感)之證候與治療。

晉隋唐 → 宋金元 → 明清Later dynasties & point counts

現代中醫的形成Modern TCM
English

TCM as practiced today is not actually all that "traditional." Before the Cultural Revolution, TCM was not a unified system — it was many systems, differing region to region and practitioner to practitioner, drawing on local traditions and herbs.

In the 1920s, Western medicine was favoured because Chinese medicine was ineffective against epidemics of TB, malaria, plague and smallpox; Yin-Yang and the Five Phases were seen as archaic and fraudulent.

In the 1950s, Mao Zedong promoted TCM to solve the lack of health care in remote regions — training "Barefoot Doctors" straight out of school in basic techniques to serve rural villages. He later had the classics translated into modern Chinese, streamlined theory, spliced in Western concepts, and promoted this new, unified system — which is what we now call Traditional Chinese Medicine.

中文

今日所行之中醫其實並非那麼「傳統」。文化大革命前,中醫並非統一體系,而是各地、各醫家自成一系,依當地傳統與藥材而異。

1920 年代,因中醫對肺結核、瘧疾、鼠疫、天花等流行病無效,西醫受青睞;陰陽與五行被視為陳舊、荒誕。

1950 年代,毛澤東推廣中醫以解決偏遠地區醫療匱乏,將剛畢業的學生速成訓練為「赤腳醫生」,以基本技術服務農村。其後將古籍譯為現代白話、精簡理論、揉入西方概念,廣泛推行此一嶄新統一體系——即今日所稱之中醫。

1.2中醫的實踐模式A Practice Model of TCM

English

TCM draws on 3,000+ years of theory and practice and aims to treat one's overall health condition, understanding symptoms as the result of complex interactions with the universe, the world, environment, profession, family, relationships and feelings.

Defining characteristics: viewing the body holistically and individualizing treatment by differentiation of pattern. Together these enable Prevention of illness, Participation of the patient, and Personalization of care (the "3 P's").

中文

中醫承襲逾 3000 年理論與實踐,著眼於治療人的整體健康狀態,理解症狀乃是人與宇宙、世界、環境、職業、家庭、人際與情緒間複雜互動之結果。

核心特徵:以整體觀看待身體,並以辨證論治個別化治療。二者共同促成疾病之預防、患者之參與,以及照護之個別化(即「三 P」)。

整體觀(Holism)——兩層意義Two meanings of Holism
English必記

(1) The human body is an organic whole. All tissues and organs are related, relying on each other for support, nourishment and function. A symptom (branch) in one place may be caused by an issue elsewhere (root); one must see the whole picture.

(2) The body in relationship with Nature. We are a component of the universe; health shifts with season, weather (damp vs hot), day/night, diet and exercise. Understanding this relationship lets us empower patients to find harmonious balance.

中文

(1) 人體是一個有機整體。各組織、臟器彼此關聯,互相支持、濡養與協調功能。某處症狀(標)可能源自他處病本(本);須綜觀全局方能明其因。

(2) 人體與自然相應。人為宇宙之一部分,健康隨季節、氣候(濕/熱)、晝夜、飲食與運動而變。明此關係,方能引導患者參與、尋得和諧平衡。

辨證論治(Bian Zheng Lun Zhi)Differentiation of syndrome
English必記

Every case is unique and constantly changing. Example — three low-back-pain patients: a 14-yr football player (sharp pain after a tackle), a 40-yr desk worker/golfer (dull pain worsening the longer she sits), a 75-yr retiree (30 years of pain/weakness, worse when it rains). To an MD they might all get Advil; to a TCM practitioner they share the same disease but each has a different pattern → a different treatment. Knowing the pattern gives the root cause, allowing more precise, effective treatment.

中文

每一病例皆獨特且不斷變化。舉例——三位下背痛患者:14 歲橄欖球員(被撞後刺痛)、40 歲久坐上班兼打高球者(愈坐愈重的鈍痛)、75 歲退休者(痛與虛弱 30 年、逢雨加劇)。若看西醫,可能都只拿到止痛藥;看中醫則病名相同、證型各異 → 治法各不相同。掌握證型即得病本,治療更精準有效。

Yin-Yang — Theory & Application

2.1陰陽的概念The Yin-Yang Concept

English

Yin-Yang is likely the single most important and distinctive theory of Chinese medicine, stemming from the Taoist philosophy of dialectics. Simple in concept but deep in application, TCM uses it to discuss how the body functions, the causes and expressions of disease, and their treatment.

中文

陰陽很可能是中醫最重要、最具特色的理論,源自道家的辯證思想。概念雖簡,運用卻深;中醫以之闡述人體功能、疾病的成因與表現,及其治療。

English必記

Contrast with the West's Aristotelian deductive reasoning ("All men are mortal; Socrates is a man; therefore Socrates is mortal") — which relies on the absolute truth of its premises. Yin-Yang is different: the two are opposites but cannot exist without each other; they rely not on absolutes but on relativity. What is Yin compared to one thing may be Yang compared to another (e.g. the "how blue is this?" example) — hence comparativeness & infinite divisibility.

中文

相對於西方以亞里斯多德演繹推理為本(「凡人皆有死;蘇格拉底是人;故蘇格拉底會死」),此推理依賴前提為絕對真。陰陽不然:二者相對而不能離彼此獨存,不憑絕對而重相對。與甲相比為陰者,與乙相比或為陽(如「這是什麼藍?」之例)——故有相對性與無限可分性。

2.2陰陽的關係(五概念)Relationship of Yin-Yang — 5 concepts

English · ① Opposition必記

1. Opposition 對立制約. Nothing escapes opposition — beware anything that seems all black or all white. Opposites are also complementary; the relationship is relative, not absolute (nothing is totally Yin or Yang). They repel/restrain each other to reach a dynamic balance; if this becomes unbalanced, excess or deficiency arises and disease follows. "If Yin predominates, Yang will be diseased; if Yang predominates, Yin will be diseased."

中文 · ① 對立

1. 對立制約。萬物無不含對立——凡看似全黑或全白者當存疑。對立者亦互補;此關係相對而非絕對(無物純陰或純陽)。二者相互排斥、制約以達動態平衡;若失衡則生偏盛偏衰,疾病乃起。「陰勝則陽病,陽勝則陰病。」

English · ② Interdependence必記

2. Interdependence 互根互用. Once an opposition is identified, Yin and Yang exist. The quality is not intrinsic — something becomes Yin or Yang only when compared to something else (water is Yang vs a stone, Yin vs fire). The presence of one allows the expression of the other (a healthy appearance reflects healthy internal organs). Yin = the material base (interior) and "guard of Yang"; Yang = the functional manifestation (exterior) and "servant of Yin."

中文 · ② 互根

2. 互根互用。對立一旦成立,陰陽即存。此性質非固有——須與他物相比才成陰或陽(水對石為陽,對火為陰)。一方之存在使另一方得以表現(外表健康反映內臟康健)。陰為(在內之)物質基礎、為「陽之守」;陽為(在外之)功能表現、為「陰之使」。

English · ③ Inter-consuming-supporting必記

3. Inter-consuming-supporting 消長平衡. As one develops, the other declines, kept within a normal range as a dynamic/relative balance (e.g. body temperature held by homeostasis). Abnormal consumption appears in pathology as: Excess of Yin, Excess of Yang, Deficiency of Yin, Deficiency of Yang.

中文 · ③ 消長

3. 消長平衡。一方漸長則另一方漸消,消長維持於常度而成動態/相對平衡(如體溫藉恆定機制維持)。異常消長於病理上表現為:陰盛、陽盛、陰虛、陽虛。

English · ④ Inter-transforming必記

4. Inter-transforming 相互轉化. When one waxes or wanes to the extreme, it turns into its opposite. This change requires specific conditions: Internal — the nature/potential must be present; External — a stimulus triggers the reaction; Time — the trigger must occur at the right moment. (Maciocia's egg→chick example.)

中文 · ④ 轉化

4. 相互轉化。一方盛衰至極則轉向其反面。此轉化須具備條件:內在——本質/潛能須存;外在——外部刺激引動;時機——刺激須於恰當時間發生。(如 Maciocia 以雞蛋孵成雛雞為喻。)

English · ⑤ Infinite divisibility必記

5. Infinite divisibility 無限可分. Yin-Yang can be further divided into Yin-Yang; any piece can be sub-divided again. Six subdivisions:

  • Greater Yin (Tai Yin) — 3rd Yin · Lesser Yin (Shao Yin) — 2nd Yin · Terminal Yin (Jue Yin) — 1st Yin
  • Greater Yang (Tai Yang) — 3rd Yang · Bright/Extreme Yang (Yang Ming) — 2nd Yang · Lesser Yang (Shao Yang) — 1st Yang
中文 · ⑤ 可分

5. 無限可分。陰陽之中復可再分陰陽;任一部分皆可再分。六分法:

  • 太陰(第三陰)·少陰(第二陰)·厥陰(第一陰)
  • 太陽(第三陽)·陽明(第二陽)·少陽(第一陽)

2.4陰陽與疾病Yin-Yang in Disease

English

Health = balance of Yin-Yang. Out of balance, signs of too much / not enough Yin or Yang appear. Two mechanisms: Preponderance (excess) and Decline (deficiency).

中文

健康即陰陽平衡。失衡則現陰陽之太過/不足。兩種機轉:偏盛(實)與偏衰(虛)。

偏盛(實)· Preponderance / Excess (Shi)
English

Preponderance of Yin → Excess (Shi) Cold. S/Sx: feeling cold (not relieved by heat), stomach/abdominal pain, acute diarrhea, pale face, pale/bluish tongue, slow pulse.

Preponderance of Yang → Excess (Shi) Heat. S/Sx: high persistent fever, red face, red tongue, rapid pulse; may decrease Yin (via sweating & thirst); secretions become viscous/darker; signs of dryness.

中文

陰偏盛 → 實寒。症狀:惡寒(得溫不減)、脘腹疼痛、急性泄瀉、面色蒼白、舌淡/青紫、脈遲。

陽偏盛 → 實熱。症狀:高熱持續、面赤、舌紅、脈數;可耗傷陰(見汗出、口渴);分泌物變稠或色深;現燥象。

偏衰(虛)· Decline / Deficiency (Xu)
English必記

Declined Yin → relative preponderance of Yang (Empty Heat 虛熱). S/Sx: low fever or subjective heat in afternoon/evening (the Yin period), red cheeks (not the whole face), night sweats, tongue red & dry without coat, pulse thin-floating-rapid; fluids scanty & darker; skin/mucous membranes dry.

03五行Five Phases
01

五行的概念

The Concept of the Five Phases
English

TCM & Chinese philosophy classify natural phenomena into five processes / phases. Commonly mistranslated in the West as "5 Elements," the theory describes processes, not the constituents of matter.

五 Wǔ = five; 行 Xíng = walk / move — so each "phase" categorizes a process. All things are grouped under Wood 木, Fire 火, Earth 土, Metal 金, Water 水.

中文

中醫與中國哲學將自然現象歸類為五種過程/相。西方常誤譯為「五種元素(5 Elements)」,實則此學說描述的是過程,而非物質構成。

五=五;行=行走、運動——故每一「行」歸納的是一種過程。萬物皆歸於木、火、土、金、水五類。

English

As with Yin-Yang, the phases move continuously from one to the next to maintain harmonious balance; they depend on and control each other. This theory explains the inter-relationships among the organs (Zang-Fu) in both physiologic function and pathologic process.

中文

如同陰陽,五行不斷由一相轉入下一相,以維持和諧平衡;彼此相依、彼此制約。此學說用以說明臟腑之間在生理功能與病理過程上的相互關係。

5.2病理關係與疾病傳變Pathologic relationships

English · abnormal Shēng (2 types)必記

Disorder transmits between "Mother-organ" and "Child-organ" in two ways:

  1. Mother fails to nourish the Child — mother is deficient, so the child becomes deficient too.
  2. Child takes too much from the Mother — child is excessive, creating deficiency in the mother.

Example (mother not nourishing child): deficient Liver-Blood → malnourishes the Heart-Shén → palpitations & insomnia.

中文 · 母子相傳(異常相生)

  1. 母不生子(母病及子)——母虛,致子亦虛。
  2. 子盜母氣(子病及母)——子過亢,反使母虛。

例(母不養子):肝血虛 → 不能濡養心神 → 心悸、失眠。

Chéng 乘 (over-controlling): a phase becomes excessive and over-consumes / disrupts the next phase along the controlling cycle. Nei Jing: if Winter over-acts on Summer, cold spells appear in summer; Kidney/Water becomes excessive and puts out Heart/Fire.

Wū 侮 (insulting): the "grandchild" phase, in excess, damages its "grandparent" phase (usually deficient) — the reverse-controlling sequence; the grandchild "insults" the grandparent.

中文 · 相乘與相侮

01

Concept & Definitions
English · concept

The channels (meridians) are a unique feature of TCM and a source of great debate — this very concept keeps many scientific thinkers skeptical of TCM. Critics note that CT/MRI/surgery found no "meridians"; but recent research into fascial planes has reignited the debate. Daniel Keown (The Spark in the Machine) traces these fascial pathways to their embryological roots, showing they follow the meridian pathways and carry bioelectrical impulses.

Analogy of a plant: the Organs (Zang-Fu) = roots, the channels = stems, the tissues & sense organs = flowers. The network links every tissue into an integrated whole, and also acts as a series of barriers against external pathogens (contained at the exterior = less serious & easier to cure).

04

綜合:病與證

Putting it all together
English必記

Symptoms = subjective (pain, nausea, blurred vision); Signs = objective (complexion, pulse, tongue). One alone means little; a cluster forms a Pattern (with cause, location, nature, & Zheng-Qi status) — usually the cause behind the manifestation.

The Western disease name ≠ the TCM disease name (the most troublesome symptom becomes the TCM "disease," e.g. endometriosis → "Abdominal Pain" / "Painful Periods" / "Infertility"). A complete diagnosis needs Disease + Pattern.

同病異治 — same disease, different treatments; 異病同治 — different diseases, same treatment.

中文
